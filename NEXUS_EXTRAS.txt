Brief overview (Nexus "Brief overview" field):
See where your stratagem ball or C4 charge will land before you throw it: a dashed arc that walls and ground hide, with throw-range armour taken into account, and C4's DEPLOY / DETONATE icon by the crosshair. Needs Chef Shared Loader 1.2+.

Changelog 1.0.3 (Nexus changelog field, one change per line):
Throws on the move: the arc now follows them. The game adds some of your speed forward or back to a throw (measured on C4 and stratagem throws); sideways movement adds none.
The arc stays up until the throw actually leaves your hand, so it doesn't vanish while the arm swings.
C4: no arc unless C4 is really in your hand (a report of an arc while aiming the Censor rifle).
Less work each frame.

Changelog 1.0.2:
Renamed from Throwable Arcs to Stratagem and C4 Trajectory. Nothing else changed; your settings are kept.

Changelog 1.0.1:
The arc now starts where your hand actually lets go, so short throws land on the arc too.
C4: the arc shows while aiming standing, crouched, prone, sliding, diving and from a vehicle's passenger seats (FRV and tank), and no longer flickers when you turn quickly.
C4: no arc in a vehicle's driver seat, and none for a moment while putting C4 away.
C4: picking up another detonator no longer leaves the mode icon on the old one.
C4 mode icon: now the game's own DEPLOY / DETONATE icons, a little smaller and further right of the crosshair; in a vehicle it shows only while you aim.
Stratagems: the arc also shows while you aim the ball with right mouse.
Below native Render Scale the arc is drawn thicker (turn Anti-Aliasing on there, or the game clips the arc to a corner of the screen).
Less work per throw: your armour is checked once instead of at every throw.

Changelog 1.0.0:
First release: arcs for stratagem balls and aimed C4, throw-range armour, C4 mode icon.
